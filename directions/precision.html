CAP_01

Agricultural operations

AirWorks operates agricultural drones for crop spraying, seeding and granular spreading, crop monitoring, multispectral and NDVI imaging, and farm mapping.

  • Application + spreading
  • Crop monitoring
  • Multispectral + NDVI
CAP_02

Mapping + survey support

Planned aerial data capture for mapping workflows. Professional survey work is carried out alongside a licensed geodetic engineer, with AirWorks operating the drone.

  • Coverage planning
  • Aerial data capture
  • Coordinated survey workflow
CAP_03

Progress monitoring

Repeatable aerial views for reviewing, comparing, and reporting how a site changes from one visit to the next.

  • Repeat viewpoints
  • Site context
  • Organised capture dates
CAP_04

Infrastructure + asset inspection

Overview and detail imagery of roads, bridges, buildings, roofs, towers, utilities, and other difficult-to-view assets for technical review.

  • Roads + bridges
  • Buildings + roofs
  • Towers + utilities
CAP_05

Aerial photo + video

Stills and movement that show a property, place, event, or project in its wider setting.

  • Photo
  • Video
  • Location context
