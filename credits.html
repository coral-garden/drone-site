Plain-language summary

What the licenses allow

  • CC BY: permits reuse, including commercial use, with creator credit, a license link, and an indication of changes.
  • CC BY-SA: has the same attribution requirements and also requires adapted versions of the image to use the same license.
  • Public domain: does not require permission or attribution, though the source is recorded here.
  • Unsplash: permits broad commercial and non-commercial use without required attribution, but does not allow selling unmodified copies or building a competing image service.

This is a practical license summary, not legal advice. The linked license text controls.
