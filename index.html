Project workflow

How projects are scoped

The brief establishes what is needed before aircraft, payload, coverage, schedule, and handoff are confirmed.

  1. 01

    Project brief

    Confirm the location, service required, intended result, target date, and known site details.

  2. 02

    Site review

    Review terrain, crop or asset details, access, weather, coordination, and operating requirements.

  3. 03

    Operation plan

    Define the aircraft, payload or sensor, coverage, viewpoints, schedule, and project responsibilities.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    Prepare the agreed files, records, imagery, or outputs for the people who will use them.

Professional survey work

AirWorks operates the drone and captures the aerial data. A licensed geodetic engineer handles the professional survey responsibilities within the agreed project workflow.
